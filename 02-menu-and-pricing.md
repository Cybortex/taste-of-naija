## Pack Definition (for Takeaway fee)

Every item from these categories counts as **one pack**:
- Any Swallow + Soup combination (or individual swallow / soup if ordered separately as a main)
- Any Rice plate
- Any Pepper Soup or Grill item
- Any Popular Combo

Proteins and Sides do **not** count as packs.

**Example**  
Customer orders:
- 2 × Pounded Yam & Egusi (2 packs)
- 1 × Fried Chicken
- 1 × Dodo

Takeaway fee = 2 × ₦250 = ₦500

## Display Rules

- Always show price next to the item name.
- Format: `₦2,500` (with comma, no decimals).
- On combo cards, show the combined price prominently and the breakdown on hover / expand.
- “Popular” badge on the seven combo items and on Party Jollof, Egusi, Beef Suya.
- “Spicy” badge on all pepper soups, Asun, Nkwobi, and Suya.
